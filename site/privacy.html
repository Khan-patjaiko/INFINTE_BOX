This policy explains what personal data [Business legal name] ("Infinite Box", "we", "us") collects through infinite-box.co, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

Infinite Box is a small 3D-printing shop operated by [Business legal name], [Registered address]. For anything related to your data, email hello@infinite-box.co.

2. What we collect

  • Account details — your email address, name and password hash when you create an account, or your name and email from Google if you sign in with Google.
  • Order details — the products you buy, the shipping address and phone number you enter at checkout, and payment confirmation from Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Custom-order requests — your name, email, phone, project description and any design files you upload.
  • Contact messages — your name, email and message when you use the contact form.
  • Cart — the items in your cart are stored only in your browser (local storage), not on our servers, until you check out.
  • Technical data — standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) kept by our hosting and backend providers for security and reliability.

3. Why we use it

  • To process and ship your orders, and to send you order confirmations and updates.
  • To prepare and send a quote for a custom order, which requires us to view your design files.
  • To answer your questions and provide support.
  • To keep your account secure and prevent fraud.
  • To meet legal obligations such as tax and accounting records.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the shop. Each only receives the data needed for its job:

  • Stripe — payment processing and collection of your shipping address at checkout (Stripe privacy policy).
  • Supabase — hosts our database, user accounts and uploaded files (Supabase privacy policy).
  • Google — only if you choose to sign in with Google (Google privacy policy).
  • Shipping carriers — your name, address and phone number so your parcel can be delivered.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

5. Design files and intellectual property

Files you upload for a custom quote are stored privately and are only accessed by our team to evaluate and produce your part. We do not share, publish or reuse your designs. You keep all rights to your designs; see our Terms of Service.

  • Account data — until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Orders and invoices — [e.g. 5 years] after the order, as required for tax and accounting.
  • Custom-order requests and uploaded files — [e.g. 12 months] after the request is closed, unless an order follows.
  • Contact messages — [e.g. 12 months] after we reply.

7. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to see the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it (unless we must keep it by law). You can also sign in and view your order history on your account page. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@infinite-box.co and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. The site uses your browser's local storage to keep your cart and, if you sign in, a session token to keep you logged in. Stripe sets its own cookies on the checkout page for fraud prevention.

9. Security

All traffic to the site is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Access to customer data and uploaded files is restricted to our team. No system is perfectly secure, so please contact us immediately if you suspect a problem with your account.

10. Children

The site is intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.
