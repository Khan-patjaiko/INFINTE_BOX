Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Infinite Box ("we", "us") collects through infinite-box.co, why we collect it, and the choices you have. It is written to comply with Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (2019) ("PDPA").

1. Who we are

Infinite Box is a small 3D-printing shop in Thailand and is the data controller for the personal data described here. Our address is 345/316 Tambon Bang Khu Wat, Mueang Pathum Thani District, Pathum Thani 12000, Thailand. For anything related to your data, email hello@infinite-box.co.

2. What we collect

  • Account details — your email address, name and password hash when you create an account, or your name and email from Google if you sign in with Google.
  • Order details — the products you buy, the shipping address and phone number you enter at checkout, and payment confirmation from Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Custom-order requests — your name, email, phone, project description and any design files you upload.
  • Contact messages — your name, email and message when you use the contact form.
  • Cart — the items in your cart are stored only in your browser (local storage), not on our servers, until you check out.
  • Technical data — standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) kept by our hosting and backend providers for security and reliability.

3. Why we use it, and on what legal basis

  • To create and run your account, process and ship your orders, and send you order confirmations and updates. Basis: performing our contract with you.
  • To prepare and send a quote for a custom order, which requires us to view your design files. Basis: steps you asked us to take before entering a contract.
  • To answer your questions and provide support. Basis: our legitimate interest in replying to you, or performing our contract with you.
  • To keep your account secure and prevent fraud. Basis: our legitimate interest in protecting customers and the shop.
  • To keep tax and accounting records. Basis: legal obligation.

If you do not give us the details needed for an order (such as a delivery address), we cannot complete it. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for third-party advertising, and we do not make automated decisions about you.

4. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the shop. Each only receives the data needed for its job:

  • Stripe — payment processing and collection of your shipping address at checkout (Stripe privacy policy).
  • Supabase — hosts our database, user accounts and uploaded files (Supabase privacy policy).
  • Google — only if you choose to sign in with Google (Google privacy policy).
  • Resend — sends our emails, such as order receipts, quote confirmations and password resets (Resend privacy policy).
  • ImprovMX — forwards email sent to hello@infinite-box.co to our inbox (ImprovMX privacy policy).
  • Hostinger — hosts the website itself (Hostinger privacy policy).
  • Shipping carriers — your name, address and phone number so your parcel can be delivered.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it, for example to a government authority or a court.

Transfers outside Thailand. Some of these providers process data outside Thailand: our database is hosted in Singapore, the website in Malaysia, and Stripe, Google, Resend and ImprovMX may process data in the United States, the European Union and other countries. We only use providers that protect personal data under their own published policies and contractual safeguards, and transfer only what each one needs to do its job.

5. Design files and intellectual property

Files you upload for a custom quote are stored privately and are only accessed by our team to evaluate and produce your part. We do not share, publish or reuse your designs. You keep all rights to your designs; see our Terms of Service.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account data — until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Orders and invoices — 5 years after the order, as required for tax and accounting.
  • Custom-order requests and uploaded files — 12 months after the request is closed, unless an order follows (then as for orders).
  • Contact messages — 12 months after we reply.

After these periods we delete the data or make it anonymous.

7. Your rights

Under the PDPA you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • receive your data in a commonly used electronic format, or have it sent to another organisation;
  • have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected;
  • have your data deleted or anonymised, unless we must keep it by law;
  • ask us to restrict how we use your data;
  • object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw any consent you have given, without affecting what we did before you withdrew it;
  • complain to the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC) of Thailand if you believe we have breached the PDPA.

You can also sign in and view your profile and order history on your account page. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@infinite-box.co. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies and local storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. The site uses your browser's local storage to keep your cart and, if you sign in, a session token to keep you logged in. Stripe sets its own cookies on the checkout page for fraud prevention.

9. Security

All traffic to the site is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text. Access to customer data and uploaded files is restricted to our team. No system is perfectly secure, so please contact us immediately if you suspect a problem with your account. If a data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will report it to the PDPC and tell you as the law requires.

10. Younger customers

Everyone is welcome to browse Infinite Box. If you are under 20, please ask a parent or guardian before creating an account or placing an order, as Thai law requires their consent. If a parent or guardian believes their child has shared personal data with us without consent, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy as the shop grows. The date at the top shows the latest version; significant changes will be noted on this page.
