Materials

The right material for the job

We stock a range of FDM filaments and resins so your part gets the properties it actually needs — not just whatever's on the spool.

PLA+

Our go-to for clean, detailed prints with minimal warping. Great for prototypes, display parts, and low-stress mechanical pieces.

PETG

Tougher and more chemical-resistant than PLA, with good layer adhesion. A solid choice for enclosures and outdoor-adjacent parts.

ABS

Heat-resistant and easy to post-process — sand, glue, or vapor-smooth. Ideal for prototypes headed toward injection-molded production.

Nylon (PA12)

Wear-resistant and low-friction, built for gears, hinges, and living mechanisms that need to move for a long time.

TPU

Flexible and durable rubber-like filament for clips, gaskets, and parts that need to bend without cracking.

Resin

SLA resin for the finest detail and smoothest surface finish — down to 0.05mm layers for miniatures and jewelry masters.
