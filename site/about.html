About

A small studio that designs parts to fit.

Infinite Box is a design and 3D printing studio in Pathum Thani, Thailand, started in 2024. We make the parts you can't buy anywhere else.

Our story

Design. Development. Fabrication.

Most of our parts start with a real problem, usually inside a car: a missing cup holder, or a phone with nowhere to sit. We measure or 3D scan the space, design the part, and print prototypes until the fit is right. Only then does it become a product in the shop.

We also take custom requests: name keychains, desk stands, replacement pieces, or anything you can describe or sketch. Send us the idea and we'll tell you what's possible.
