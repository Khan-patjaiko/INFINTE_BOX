Our story

From a garage printer to a full production floor

Infinite Box began as a single FDM printer running late nights for local makers who needed one-off brackets and enclosures nobody else would touch in small quantities. As demand grew, so did our floor — resin printers for detail work, a wider material library, and a process built specifically around short runs and custom specs.

Today we print everything from mounting hardware for hardware startups to display parts for retail brands — always with the same promise: send us your design, and we'll treat it like our own.
